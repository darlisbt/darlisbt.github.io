Hi, there! I'm Darlis. Since 2019, I am the Digital Communications and Web Specialist of the FAU MoD, Research Center for Mathematics of Data and of the FAU DCN-AvH, Chair for Dynamics, Control, Machine Learning and Numerics – Alexander von Humboldt Professorship headed by Prof. Enrique Zuazua at FAU, Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (Germany).

In 2023 I completed, the Machine Learning Specialization (Stanford University and DeepLearning.AI, 2023) and a Google AI Professional Certificate (2026).

I'm a Computer Science Engineer (UCAB) and hold Master degrees in: Internet Business, Digital Design & 3D Animation and Cisco CCNA Telecom & IT Essentials (Basque Country, Spain).

I am proud to have worked on the typesettings and English translation of the scientific book by Prof. E. Zuazua (2024): Exact Controllability and Stabilization of the Wave Equation, Springer https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-58857-0, FAU CRIS

I'm passionate about finding creative ways to support and promote our team's research—whether through enhanced web rendering of mathematical content, organizing scientific events, producing digital media and scientific outreach:

+82 Mathematical content (LaTeX) typesetting & web rendering

+100 Scientific events coordination, organization & logistics (see: FAU MoD Lectures • FAU MoD Courses/workshops • FAU DCN-AvH Seminars)

+100 Open Science & Scientific communication sources: posters, flyers, media/web design, scientific dissemination, newsletter
